# CS 255: System Analysis and Design

## DriverPass Project

This repository contains my work from CS 255 at Southern New Hampshire University. It includes my business requirements document from Project One and my system design document from Project Two.

### Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client was DriverPass, a company that wanted to help students prepare for their driving tests. They needed an online system where customers could purchase training packages, schedule driving lessons, and take practice tests. The system also needed to allow employees to manage reservations, track student progress, and access reports.

### What did you do particularly well?

I think I did well at identifying the different requirements and organizing them into a system design. I was able to break down the features DriverPass needed and show how different users would interact with the system through UML diagrams.

### If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

I would improve my UML diagrams by making them more detailed and easier to read. Some of the diagrams could use better spacing and clearer connections between different parts of the system. I would also make sure they follow UML standards more closely so other developers can understand them without confusion.

### How did you interpret the user's needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user's needs when designing?

I interpreted the client's needs by reviewing the DriverPass interview and identifying the features they wanted. I used those requirements to design the scheduling process, user roles, training packages, and other system functions. Considering user needs is important because even if a system works technically, it won't be successful if it doesn't solve the problems users actually have.

### How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

I approach software design by first understanding the problem and identifying what the system needs to accomplish. From there, I break the requirements into smaller parts and use diagrams to visualize how everything should work. In the future, I would continue using UML diagrams, gathering feedback, and reviewing requirements throughout the design process to make sure the final system meets the client's expectations.
